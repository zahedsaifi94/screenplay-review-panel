# Scene chart

For the Structure specialist. Fill it in for every scene before writing the review, attach it to the review, and cite scene numbers in debate items. Shorts: one row per scene. Features: one row per scene, grouped by sequence; you may summarize a montage as one row.

## The table

| # | Page | Who drives | Wants | What opposes | State at start | State at end | Emotional note |
|---|------|-----------|-------|--------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| 1 | 1 | Nora | to clear the fridge | her own hesitation; Dev's impatience | containers in fridge, lamp on | soup in hand, lamp off | tender / stalling |

- **Who drives:** the character whose want moves the scene. "Nobody" is an answer, and a flag.
- **Wants / What opposes:** in the scene, not in the film. If you can't name an opposition, the scene has no conflict.
- **State at start / end:** a fact, a balance, a relationship, an object. If the two match, nothing turned.
- **Emotional note:** one or two words for what the scene plays as (tender, tense, funny, flat).

## How to read it

Look down the columns, not across the rows:

- **Non-events.** Rows where start and end match. Cut or rewrite candidates; say which.
- **Repeated beats.** Two rows with the same want, same opposition, same level of pressure. The second one usually goes, or needs to escalate.
- **Flat runs.** Three or more rows with the same emotional note. Vary one, or merge.
- **Passive stretches.** Several rows in a row where the protagonist is not the driver. Fine for a deliberate stretch; a flag after the first turn.
- **Vanished threads.** (Features) A character or question that disappears for a long run of rows and returns unannounced.
- **Pointing at the ending.** Mark each row that points toward the last image. Rows that don't are the first place to look for cuts.

Report the findings in the review as a short list under "Scene chart findings," then go scene by scene as usual. Do not present the raw table to the writer unless they ask; the orchestrator keeps it.
