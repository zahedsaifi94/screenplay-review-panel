                          NIGHT SHIFT

                          Written by

                          Test Writer




                      Feature. 95 pages. Drama.
         Sample excerpts; bracketed markers give the pages of
                 the full script each excerpt comes from.




[pp. 1-10]

FADE IN:

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - BASEMENT CORRIDOR - NIGHT

A long corridor the color of old teeth. Fluorescent tubes,
one of them ticking. A line painted on the floor: yellow for
Theatres, blue for the Mortuary.

JONAH REID (54), porter's tunic, a lanyard with a photo ten
years younger, pushes an empty bed along the yellow line. He
walks like a man who knows exactly how many steps this is.

He stops at a vending machine. Doesn't buy anything. Taps
the glass twice with his knuckle. Keeps walking.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - PORTERS' LODGE - NIGHT

A room with no windows. A kettle, a whiteboard, a radio set
with a cracked face. A wall clock reads 10:52 PM.

MARISOL VEGA (23), new tunic, creases still in it, stares at
a laminated map of the hospital.

                    MARISOL
          Where's Ward Eleven?

                    JONAH
          There isn't one.

                    MARISOL
          It's on the sheet.

                    JONAH
          It's on the sheet because nobody's
          taken it off the sheet. It's been a
          storeroom since 2019.

She looks at the map again. He takes the sheet from her,
crosses something out with a biro, hands it back.

                    JONAH (CONT'D)
          Bloods go to Path on the hour. Not
          five past. On the hour.

                    MARISOL
          And if a ward calls at five past?

                    JONAH
          Then they wait till the next hour,
          and they'll tell you it's urgent, and
          it never is.

The radio crackles.

                    RADIO (V.O.)
          Porter to A and E, trolley transfer,
          bay four.

Jonah reaches for the radio. Stops. Nods at Marisol.

                    JONAH
          Go on.

                    MARISOL
          On my own?

                    JONAH
          Bay four. Blue doors. You'll hear it
          before you see it.

She goes. He fills the kettle.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - A&E - BAY FOUR - NIGHT

Curtains. A man on a trolley, ARTHUR PELL (78), oxygen mask,
eyes closed. A NURSE hands Marisol a folder.

                    NURSE
          Pell. Going up to Respiratory. Bed's
          ready.

                    MARISOL
          Respiratory's --

                    NURSE
          Third floor. Lift B.

The nurse is already gone. Marisol looks at the folder, at
the man, at the two identical corridors leading away.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - PORTERS' LODGE - NIGHT

Jonah, tea in hand, watches the clock tick to 11:00. He
picks up a tray of blood samples in sealed bags and leaves.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - PATHOLOGY HATCH - NIGHT

A hatch in a wall. Jonah slides the tray through. A hand
takes it. No face.

His phone buzzes in his tunic pocket. He looks. UNKNOWN
NUMBER. He lets it ring out.

It buzzes again. Same number. He answers.

                    JONAH
          Reid.

                    VOICE (V.O.)
                (filtered)
          Mr. Reid? This is the emergency
          department at St. Brendan's. Are you
          the father of Tess Reid?

A long beat. Jonah looks down the corridor he's standing in.

                    JONAH
          I'm in the building.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - A&E - RESUS - NIGHT

Bright. Loud. TESS REID (19), a hospital gown over a party
top, an oxygen mask, a cannula in her hand. Her eyes find
Jonah in the doorway. She pulls the mask down.

                    TESS
          Don't.

                    JONAH
          I haven't said anything.

                    TESS
          You're doing the face.

A doctor, DR. OKONJO (40s), steps between them, clipboard up.

                    DR. OKONJO
          Dad? She's stable. We think she took
          something at a party, we don't know
          what yet. We're keeping her tonight.

                    JONAH
          Took what?

                    DR. OKONJO
          That's what the bloods will tell us.
          They've gone down to Path.

Jonah looks at his watch. 11:14.

                    JONAH
          They won't have gone yet.

                    DR. OKONJO
          Sorry?

                    JONAH
          Nothing.

He turns to go.

                    TESS
          Where are you going?

                    JONAH
          To get your bloods to the lab.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - STAIRWELL - NIGHT

Jonah takes the stairs two at a time. His radio crackles.

                    RADIO (V.O.)
          Any porter, any porter. Respiratory
          reporting a patient not received.
          Pell, Arthur. Transferred from A and E
          at twenty-three oh two.

Jonah stops on the landing.

                    RADIO (V.O.) (CONT'D)
          Any porter.

He looks up the stairwell. Then down.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - LIFT B LOBBY - SECOND FLOOR - NIGHT

Marisol stands beside Arthur Pell's trolley in front of a
pair of locked double doors. A sign: ORTHOPAEDICS - NO
ACCESS AFTER 9PM. She is pressing a buzzer that doesn't
buzz.

Arthur's oxygen mask has fogged and cleared, fogged and
cleared. The gauge on the cylinder under the trolley sits
in the red.

Jonah comes round the corner at a run. Stops. Takes it in:
the floor number, the sign, the gauge.

                    MARISOL
          It said third floor. The lift stopped
          here and the doors --

                    JONAH
          How long.

                    MARISOL
          I don't --

                    JONAH
          How long has he been on this
          cylinder.

                    MARISOL
          Since the bay.

He crouches. Reads the gauge. Reads Arthur's face.

                    JONAH
          Mr. Pell. Arthur. Can you hear me?

Arthur's eyes open. Find him. Close again.

Jonah stands. Looks at Marisol. Looks at the radio on his
own belt.

He picks up the radio.

                    JONAH (CONT'D)
                (into radio)
          Porter Reid. I've got Pell. Lift
          fault on B, we're on two. Bringing
          him up the service lift now. Two
          minutes.

                    RADIO (V.O.)
          Received, Reid.

He clips the radio back. Marisol stares at him.

                    MARISOL
          There wasn't a lift fault.

                    JONAH
          There is now. Push.


[pp. 24-30]

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - RESPIRATORY WARD - NURSES' STATION - NIGHT

Arthur Pell is in a bed through the glass, a fresh mask on,
a monitor beeping steadily. The CHARGE NURSE, GRETA LINDQVIST
(50s), reading glasses on a chain, writes in a ledger.

                    GRETA
          Twenty-three oh two from A and E.
          Received twenty-three thirty-one.
          That's twenty-nine minutes, Jonah.

                    JONAH
          Lift B.

                    GRETA
          Lift B's been fine all week.

                    JONAH
          It wasn't fine at eleven.

She looks at him over the glasses. Then at Marisol, who
stands a step behind him, hands clasped like a child at a
funeral.

                    GRETA
          Who was pushing?

A beat.

                    JONAH
          I was.

                    GRETA
          You were on bloods at eleven. I saw
          the tray go through.

                    JONAH
          I did the bloods and then I did Pell.

                    GRETA
          In the same minute.

                    JONAH
          I'm quick.

Greta holds his eye for a long moment. She writes something.

                    GRETA
          His sats were seventy-nine when he
          came in here. I'm putting that in.
          And I'm putting in the lift.

                    JONAH
          Put in whatever you saw.

                    GRETA
          I'm putting in what you told me. Which
          isn't the same thing, and we both know
          it.

She closes the ledger.

                    GRETA (CONT'D)
          Estates will check the lift log in
          the morning.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - SERVICE CORRIDOR - NIGHT

Jonah and Marisol walk. Fast. He doesn't look at her.

                    MARISOL
          Why did you say that?

                    JONAH
          Which part.

                    MARISOL
          Any of it.

                    JONAH
          Because if I say you did it, you're
          gone by breakfast, and if I say I did
          it, I get a form.

                    MARISOL
          You don't know me.

                    JONAH
          I know you've got the creases still
          in your tunic.

He stops at a door marked ESTATES - AUTHORISED ONLY. Looks
both ways. Takes out a key that isn't on his lanyard.

                    MARISOL
          What are you doing?

                    JONAH
          Lift log.

                    MARISOL
          You can't --

                    JONAH
          Go and sit with Mr. Pell. If he asks
          who brought him up, you say Jonah.

                    MARISOL
          And if he asks who left him on the
          wrong floor?

                    JONAH
          He won't. He was asleep.

                    MARISOL
          He opened his eyes.

Jonah looks at her. The key stays in the lock, unturned.

                    JONAH
          Go and sit with him.

She goes. He turns the key.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - A&E - OBSERVATION BAY - NIGHT

Tess, in a bed now, scrolling her phone with the hand that
doesn't have a cannula in it. Jonah pulls the curtain
aside. His hands are black with something: grease, dust.

                    TESS
          You look like you climbed out of a
          grave.

                    JONAH
          Lift shaft.

                    TESS
          Is that a porter thing?

                    JONAH
          It is tonight.

He sits. She doesn't look up from the phone.

                    JONAH (CONT'D)
          What did you take?

                    TESS
          Dr. Okonjo asked me that.

                    JONAH
          And?

                    TESS
          And I told Dr. Okonjo.

                    JONAH
          Tess.

                    TESS
          You've got work. Go and do work.

He stands. Stops at the curtain.

                    JONAH
          Your mum would've --

                    TESS
          Don't do that either.

He goes.


[pp. 47-52]

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - PORTERS' LODGE - NIGHT (LATER)

The clock reads 2:40 AM. Marisol asleep in a chair, head
against the map. Jonah at the table with a printout: a lift
maintenance log, one line altered in a different pen.

The radio.

                    RADIO (V.O.)
          Crash team to Respiratory, bed six.
          Crash team to Respiratory, bed six.

Marisol wakes. They look at each other. Neither moves.

                    MARISOL
          Which bed was he?

Jonah is already out of the door.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - RESPIRATORY WARD - CORRIDOR - NIGHT

Jonah arrives at a run and stops. Through the glass of bed
six: a team around Arthur Pell, a nurse on his chest, Greta
at the foot of the bed with her glasses off.

Jonah stands in the corridor where porters stand. He is not
needed. He doesn't leave.

The team slows. Stops. Someone looks at the clock.

Greta turns and sees Jonah through the glass. She holds his
eye. Then she puts her glasses back on and picks up the
ledger.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - RESPIRATORY WARD - SLUICE ROOM - NIGHT

A small room, steel sinks. Greta and Jonah. The door shut.

                    GRETA
          He'd have gone tonight anyway. That's
          what Okonjo will say and he might be
          right.

                    JONAH
          Might.

                    GRETA
          Twenty-nine minutes on a cylinder
          that was in the red when he left A
          and E. That's what I'm going to be
          asked about. And the lift.

                    JONAH
          Then tell them about the lift.

                    GRETA
          Estates called me back at one. The
          log says fault at twenty-three oh
          five, cleared twenty-three twenty.

                    JONAH
          There you are then.

                    GRETA
          Jonah. The log is on paper. In a
          cupboard. In a room you don't have
          a key to.

A beat.

                    GRETA (CONT'D)
          There'll be a review. Thursday,
          probably. Me, Okonjo, someone from
          the Trust. You'll be asked to attend.
          So will the girl.

                    JONAH
          She wasn't there.

                    GRETA
          Then she'll have a very short
          meeting.

She opens the door. Stops.

                    GRETA (CONT'D)
          Your daughter's bloods came back.
          Okonjo's looking for you.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - A&E - DOCTORS' OFFICE - NIGHT

Dr. Okonjo, a printout, two chairs. Jonah doesn't sit.

                    DR. OKONJO
          It wasn't a party drug. It was
          paracetamol. A lot of it. About six
          hours before she came in.

Jonah sits.

                    DR. OKONJO (CONT'D)
          We've started her on the antidote.
          Her liver numbers are up but we
          caught it in the window. Another two
          or three hours and we'd be having a
          different conversation.

                    JONAH
          Who brought her in?

                    DR. OKONJO
          A friend. Girl called Priya. She's
          been in the waiting room since
          eleven. She asked me not to tell you
          she was here.

                    JONAH
          Why?

                    DR. OKONJO
          I think you'd have to ask Tess that.

Jonah looks at his hands. Still grey with dust in the
creases.

                    DR. OKONJO (CONT'D)
          Mr. Reid. Did you know she was
          struggling?

                    JONAH
          I work nights.

                    DR. OKONJO
          That's not what I asked.

                    JONAH
          I know it isn't.


[pp. 70-75]

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - A&E - OBSERVATION BAY - DAWN

Grey light through a high window. Tess asleep. PRIYA (19),
coat on, sitting on the floor against the wall with her
knees up. Jonah in the chair.

                    PRIYA
                (quiet)
          She said you'd fix it. That's why she
          didn't want you told. She said you'd
          come in and fix it and she'd have to
          watch you do it.

                    JONAH
          Fix what?

                    PRIYA
          Her. Everything. She said you can't
          stand a thing being wrong in front
          of you.

Jonah says nothing.

                    PRIYA (CONT'D)
          She's been not okay since March.

                    JONAH
          I'd have noticed.

                    PRIYA
          You work nights.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - PORTERS' LODGE - DAY

Daylight has never reached this room, but the clock says
7:50 AM and the day porters are arriving. Marisol, tunic
now creased in the ordinary way, stands with her bag.

                    MARISOL
          I've been called for Thursday.

                    JONAH
          Greta said.

                    MARISOL
          What do I say?

                    JONAH
          You say you were on A and E bays and
          you didn't see Pell leave.

                    MARISOL
          That's not what happened.

                    JONAH
          It's what's written down.

                    MARISOL
          You wrote it down.

He doesn't answer. She puts her bag over her shoulder.

                    MARISOL (CONT'D)
          He opened his eyes, Jonah. On the
          second floor. He looked at me. I'm
          not going to sit in a room and say
          I wasn't there.

                    JONAH
          Then you'll lose the job.

                    MARISOL
          Then I'll lose the job.

                    JONAH
          And I'll lose mine for the lift log,
          and Greta'll have it in writing that
          I lied to her face. That's the trade.
          That's what you're buying with the
          truth.

                    MARISOL
          I didn't ask you to buy anything.

She goes. The door swings. Jonah looks at the whiteboard:
his name against every job since 10:52 last night. Pell's
transfer in his handwriting.

He picks up the cloth and wipes the board.

Stops with half of it gone.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - A&E - OBSERVATION BAY - DAY

Tess awake. Priya gone. Jonah in the doorway, tunic off, a
jacket over his arm.

                    TESS
          Shift's over.

                    JONAH
          Shift's over.

                    TESS
          Are you going home?

                    JONAH
          I'm going to sit here. If that's
          allowed.

                    TESS
          It's a free hospital.

He sits. A long silence. Then:

                    JONAH
          A man died tonight. On my shift. He
          was on the wrong floor for half an
          hour and I said it was a lift.

Tess looks at him. Properly, for the first time.

                    TESS
          Was it a lift?

                    JONAH
          No.

                    TESS
          Why are you telling me?

                    JONAH
          Because you said I can't stand a
          thing being wrong in front of me.

                    TESS
          Priya said that.

                    JONAH
          Priya said you said it.

                    TESS
          It's true though.

                    JONAH
          I know.

She turns her hand over on the blanket. The cannula. He
looks at it and not at her face.

                    JONAH (CONT'D)
          I didn't fix you. I didn't even see
          you.

                    TESS
          You're seeing me now.

                    JONAH
          Is that enough?

                    TESS
          I don't know. Ask me Thursday.


[pp. 88-95]

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - TRUST MEETING ROOM - DAY

A table too big for the room. Water jug. Greta, Dr. Okonjo,
and a Trust officer, MR. HALE (60s), a laptop open. Jonah in
a shirt that has been ironed by someone who doesn't iron.

                    MR. HALE
          Mr. Reid, the transfer record shows
          you collected Mr. Pell from the
          emergency department at twenty-three
          oh two. Is that correct?

Jonah looks at Greta. She doesn't look away. She doesn't
help.

                    JONAH
          No.

                    MR. HALE
          I'm sorry?

                    JONAH
          Ms. Vega collected him. I was on
          bloods. She took him to the wrong
          floor because the sheet she'd been
          given was out of date, which I knew,
          and I'd sent her on her own, which I
          shouldn't have.

                    MR. HALE
          The transfer record --

                    JONAH
          I wrote the transfer record. And I
          altered the lift log in the Estates
          cupboard with a key I'm not supposed
          to have.

Silence. Okonjo sits back. Hale stops typing.

                    MR. HALE
          You understand what you're telling
          me.

                    JONAH
          I've had since Thursday morning to
          understand it.

                    GRETA
          Why now?

Jonah looks at her.

                    JONAH
          Because she was going to sit in that
          chair and tell you anyway. And I'd
          rather she heard it from me first.
          And because --

He stops. Starts again.

                    JONAH (CONT'D)
          Because I've spent twenty years
          making sure nothing's wrong in front
          of me, and something was wrong behind
          me the whole time, and I didn't look.

                    MR. HALE
          I don't follow.

                    JONAH
          You don't need to.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - MAIN ENTRANCE - DAY

Automatic doors. Jonah comes out into actual daylight. He
squints at it like something he's heard about.

Marisol on a bench, tunic on, waiting for her own turn.

                    MARISOL
          Well?

                    JONAH
          You'll be fine. Tell them exactly
          what happened. All of it.

                    MARISOL
          And you?

                    JONAH
          I told them exactly what happened.

She understands. She stands up.

                    MARISOL
          Jonah --

                    JONAH
          Bloods go on the hour. Not five past.

He walks on.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - A&E - OBSERVATION BAY - DAY

Tess dressed, sitting on the edge of the bed, a discharge
letter in her hand. Jonah in the doorway, no lanyard.

                    TESS
          They said I can go.

                    JONAH
          I'll take you.

                    TESS
          You're not working?

                    JONAH
          No.

                    TESS
          Tonight?

                    JONAH
          Not tonight. Not for a while.

She looks at him. At the empty place on his chest where the
lanyard was.

                    TESS
          Was that the thing? The lift thing?

                    JONAH
          That was the thing.

She stands. Picks up her bag. He takes it from her. She
lets him.

INT. ST. BRENDAN'S HOSPITAL - BASEMENT CORRIDOR - DAY

The same corridor. Daylight doesn't reach here either, but
the tube that was ticking has been replaced; the light is
steady.

Jonah and Tess walk along the yellow line toward the exit.
He is not pushing anything.

They pass the vending machine. He doesn't tap it.

Tess stops. Goes back. Taps the glass twice with her
knuckle. Looks at him.

                    TESS
          What's that for?

                    JONAH
          I don't know. I've always done it.

                    TESS
          Does it work?

                    JONAH
          Nothing's ever come out.

She taps it again. Nothing comes out. They walk on.

FADE OUT.
