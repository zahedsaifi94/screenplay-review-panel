                          Written by

                          Test Writer




FADE IN:

INT. DAD'S APARTMENT - KITCHEN - NIGHT

A narrow kitchen. A fridge covered in takeout menus. A brass
lamp glows by the window.

NORA (40s), coat still on, phone to her ear, opens the
fridge. Rows of plastic containers. Each lid: masking tape, a
date, his handwriting.

                    NORA
          They're all labeled, Dev.

                    DEV (V.O.)
                (on phone)
          Labeled how?

                    NORA
          Dates. "Soup. Tuesday." He labeled
          the soup.

                    DEV (V.O.)
          Throw it out.

She takes out one container. Holds it.

                    NORA
          It's lentil.

                    DEV (V.O.)
          Nora.

She switches the lamp off at the wall. Dark, except the
fridge light.

                    NORA
          I'm doing it. I'm doing it now.

She stacks the containers into a cardboard box. One by one.

The lamp clicks back on by itself.

9:00 PM.

She looks at it. Picks up the box with both hands.

                    NORA
          He set a timer.

                    DEV (V.O.)
          Turn it off.

She sets the box down. Opens the fridge. Puts the soup back.

FADE OUT.
