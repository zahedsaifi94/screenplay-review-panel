# LEFTOVERS: decisions file

## Goals
Format: short. Logline: A grieving daughter, clearing out her late father's fridge under her brother's phone pressure, finds his lamp timer still running and can't throw the soup out. Length ceiling: under 5 pages. Tone: quiet comedy-drama. Targets: short film festivals. Production scale: one location, one on-camera actor.

## Intent (orchestrator only)
Nora can't let go of her father's routines. The lamp on its timer is his routine still running without him. Dev isn't cold: he grieves by getting things done, and the film doesn't take sides between them. She keeps the soup.
- Controlling idea: letting go fails because his care is still running the house. Counter-idea: getting things done is also love.
- Intended effect: ache.

## Deliberate choices (don't flag)
1. Dev is never seen; he is a voice on the phone.
2. The ending: she puts the soup back, with no line after it.

## Rejected ideas (don't re-propose)

- A photo of Dad on the fridge.
